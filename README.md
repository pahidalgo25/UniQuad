# UniQuad

A website that is designed for seemless integration with any university without interfering with existing systems or processes, to ensure that students can connect with eachother on campus following the universities' guidelines and policies. To achieve seemless integration, UniQuad aims to utilize existing system and information that the university already stores.

UniQuad is built to be lightweight and fast to make club registration easier, finding clubs effortless, and moderating effiecent. By recognizing the already existing systems that a campus has, UniQuad will not override or replace many of those systems, making transitions quick and inexpensive.

Student accounts offer access to the club registry and club creation all in one with a simple one page dashboard. Club submissions are sent to the universities' respective offices and are determined acceptable in accordance to their policies. 

Admin accounts are utilized by any given campus' department that is responsible for approving and moderating clubs. UniQuad offers tools to make sure that a club is following and will continue to follow university rules.
